05Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Is the $200 inspection fee refundable?
Yes. It is refunded in full in two situations: if the property fails our inspection and you walk away, or when you complete the purchase. It is the only payment taken before you receive the property report.
How does the $5,000 monthly plan actually work?
The instalment is fixed at $5,000 regardless of the home. The number of months is simply the catalogue price divided by $5,000 and rounded up. A $25,000 home runs 5 months; an $80,000 home runs 16 months. There is no interest, no arrangement fee and no indexation.
What discount do I get for paying in bulk?
4% off the catalogue price. On a $128,000 home that is a saving of $5,120. Bulk buyers also receive the fastest timeline: title deeds and keys within 21 working days of cleared funds.
Can I buy from outside the United States?
Absolutely — distance is not a barrier. Roughly seven in ten of our buyers are outside the country where the property sits. Everything from the video walkthrough to the deed courier is designed for remote purchase, and all prices are quoted in US dollars.
What is the cheapest home you sell?
Our catalogue starts at $25,000 for a completed, title-verified home with two bedrooms and two bathrooms. Entry-level properties are typically two-bedroom houses in regional towns, and they are subject to the same inspection standard as our villas.
When do I get the keys?
Bulk buyers at transfer, usually 21 working days after funds clear. Monthly plan buyers at 60% of the balance paid, with full title transferred once the final instalment settles.
What happens if a property fails inspection?
The file closes, you receive the written report explaining the fault, and every payment — including the $200 fee — is returned. We would rather lose a sale than hand over a home we would not live in ourselves.

1. Scope

This Payment Policy explains how payments are collected, applied, receipted and refunded by Vanguard Estates. It forms part of our Terms of Service and applies to all property enquiries, inspections and purchases made through this website, by telephone or by text message. If a signed sale agreement contains different payment terms, the sale agreement applies.

2. Currency and pricing

All prices and payments are in United States dollars (USD). Catalogue prices start at $25,000 for a completed, title-verified home. The quoted price covers the property, our legal work on the transfer, registry filing and couriered title documents, unless your written quotation says otherwise. Any local transfer tax, stamp duty or bank charge that applies in the destination country will be identified in writing before you sign. We never add an undisclosed closing fee after signature.

3. The $200 inspection fee

A flat $200 is payable for each property you wish to inspect. It is the only amount required before you receive the property report, and it covers:

  • A physical condition survey by our own or instructed surveyor.
  • A title registry search for ownership, liens and planning status.
  • A narrated 4K video walkthrough of the property, plot and street.
  • A written report delivered within five working days.

The fee is fully refunded when your purchase completes, or where the property fails inspection and we cannot remedy the fault, or where we withdraw the property. If the report is clean and you decide not to proceed for your own reasons, the fee is retained to cover the survey. No deposit and no further amount is due at inspection stage.

4. Monthly plan — $5,000 per instalment

The monthly instalment is fixed at $5,000 regardless of the property. The term is the catalogue price divided by $5,000, rounded up to a whole month:

  • A $25,000 home — 5 instalments, settled in full after 5 months.
  • A $52,000 home — 11 instalments, settled in full after 11 months.
  • A $128,000 home — 26 instalments, settled in full after 26 months.

No interest is charged. The instalment is never indexed or reviewed upwards during your term. There is no arrangement fee, no deposit and no early redemption penalty. Where the price is not an exact multiple of $5,000, the final instalment is reduced so that the total paid never exceeds the rounded-up plan total by more than the difference stated in your agreement. Keys and occupancy are released once 60% of the balance has been paid; full registered title follows final settlement.

5. Bulk payment — 4% discount

If you settle the catalogue price in one payment, we reduce it by 4%. Example: a $128,000 home settles at $122,880, a saving of $5,120. The $200 inspection fee is refunded on completion. Title transfer is prepared immediately and keys are handed over within 21 working days of cleared funds, subject only to registry processing times in the destination country.

6. Accepted payment methods

  • Bank transfer (wire): to the corporate account named in your written payment instructions. Always confirm the account details by calling +1 (917) 999-1592 before sending funds.
  • Escrow: funds held by a licensed third-party escrow agent until the deed is registered. Recommended for bulk purchases and international buyers.
  • Bank draft or cashier's cheque: drawn in US dollars and made payable to Vanguard Estates.
  • Card payment: arranged through our Houston office for the $200 inspection fee and, where permitted, bulk settlement. Card payments may be subject to a processing charge disclosed at the time.

We do not accept payment to personal accounts, money-transfer apps, cryptocurrency or cash by post. If anyone gives you different instructions, stop and contact us immediately.

7. Escrow and receipts

Payments made under a sale agreement are acknowledged within one working day by email, with a numbered receipt showing the amount, currency, property reference and how the payment has been applied. Bulk funds are held in escrow until the transfer documents are lodged. Only then are funds released to Vanguard Estates. Your inspection fee receipt will state the refund conditions in writing.

8. Missed instalments and payment pauses

Life happens, and we would rather help than penalise. Every monthly-plan client may pause payments once per calendar year for up to 60 days, without charge or interest. Notify us in writing before the instalment due date. Beyond that pause, if an instalment remains unpaid for 30 days after its due date, we will contact you to agree a revised schedule. Your agreement may provide for cancellation if an alternative schedule cannot be agreed, with refunds calculated under section 10.

9. Early settlement

If you are on the monthly plan and wish to settle early, you may pay the outstanding balance at any time. Where you settle the remainder in one payment, the same 4% reduction applies to the outstanding amount. Your title documents are then released as soon as the settlement clears.

10. Refunds and cancellations

Refunds are processed within 20 working days of approval and returned by the original method where possible, otherwise by transfer to an account in your name.
